Data Processing Notice

This notice explains how limited user data is handled within the NoctAI platform.


1. Scope of Data Collection

NoctAI collects only the minimum information required to facilitate participation and operational communication. This may include:

  • an email address provided by the user;
  • transaction identifiers (TxID) submitted for verification purposes;
  • technical metadata generated during platform interaction.

No sensitive personal data or identity documents are requested or processed.


2. Purpose of Processing

The collected data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • verification of submitted transactions;
  • confirmation and fixation of participation records;
  • communication related to platform activity and participation status.

Data is not used for marketing, profiling, or unsolicited communications.


3. Data Storage and Access

User-provided data is stored in a controlled environment and accessed only by authorized personnel for operational purposes.

NoctAI does not sell, rent, or disclose user data to third parties, except where required by applicable law.


4. User Responsibility

Users provide information voluntarily and confirm that the submitted data is accurate and relevant.

NoctAI is not responsible for incorrect or incomplete information provided by users.


5. No Data Guarantees

While reasonable technical and organizational measures are applied, NoctAI does not guarantee absolute data security and shall not be held liable for circumstances beyond its reasonable control.


6. Acknowledgement

By submitting information through the platform, users acknowledge and accept the terms of this Data Processing Notice.
