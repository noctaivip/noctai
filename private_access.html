Private Capital Access

Access to Private Opportunities

This is the unified private access layer of the NoctAI platform: selected private projects, high-value infrastructure opportunities, and access qualification combined into one controlled page.

Access is available only to investors who meet the private capital participation threshold. The opportunities below are shown with intentionally limited disclosure. Full materials, deeper transaction structure, and extended project data remain restricted until manual approval.

Access threshold
$250,000+

Total verified participation across platform opportunities, not a single transaction.

Access format
Manual review

Access is discretionary, not automatic, and may be declined without explanation.

Participation structure

Access capital is not a fee. Capital is allocated into a selected project after approval.

Base participation
$250,000 → ~1%

Initial participation grants approximately 1% in a selected project after allocation.

Extended allocation
Depends on round structure

Extended participation is determined by the structure of the selected project. As a standard framework, each round represents 12% of project allocation. Example: an $8,000,000 round represents 12% allocation; a $12,000,000 round represents 12% allocation.

Final allocation depends on project selection, round structure, and availability at the time of approval.

Access qualification

What is evaluated

Total capital exposure is considered, not a single transaction. The platform evaluates participation level, allocation quality, and alignment with the private access layer.

What access provides

Access to closed and future opportunities, early visibility into selected rounds, private updates, and limited deal materials.

Request private access

For manual review, please provide a contact email and Transaction Hash (TxID) confirming your participation and qualification for access to the platform’s private capital opportunities.

All requests are reviewed manually. Access may be declined without explanation. This page does not constitute a public offer or guarantee of participation.
