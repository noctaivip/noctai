All participation is executed outside the platform through direct legal agreements between founders and investors.

Deal structure

Each project defines its own participation structure:

• Allocation size and valuation
• Minimum participation threshold
• Legal agreement format
• Strategic conditions

The platform standardizes presentation, but does not standardize deal terms.

Admission criteria

Projects are admitted only after internal review:

• Verified legal entity
• Defined ownership structure
• Documented equity allocation
• Founder identity confirmation

Unverified or unstructured offers are not listed.

Platform positioning

NoctAI is not a broker, marketplace or investment platform.

It is a controlled access layer between founders offering equity and investors seeking structured participation.

The platform does not process capital, does not execute transactions and does not provide investment advice.
