NoctAI™


Global marketplace for private company equity

NoctAI is a marketplace where founders can offer a minority stake in their companies and where investors gain access to private equity opportunities normally unavailable outside closed venture networks. The platform connects founders seeking strategic capital with investors interested in acquiring equity in high-growth private companies.

Participation terms are defined individually for each project and are disclosed on the project’s dedicated page.

How the platform works

For founders

List a minority equity stake in your company and connect with global investors. Raise capital without relying on traditional venture networks.

For investors

Discover private companies offering equity participation. Access curated opportunities and connect directly with founders.

Direct deal structure

NoctAI does not process investments or hold funds. All transactions are executed directly between founders and investors through separate legal agreements.

Platform requirements

Companies listed on NoctAI must meet several requirements:

• Registered legal entity
• Clear equity structure
• Verified company ownership
• Transparent documentation of the offered stake

Projects that do not meet these criteria are not admitted to the platform.

Platform model

NoctAI acts as a discovery platform for private equity opportunities.

The platform does not sell securities, does not act as a broker, and does not process investment transactions.

Investment agreements are executed directly between founders and investors outside the platform.

Control and Governance

All projects and investments on the platform are subject to centralized management by NoctAI.

The platform oversees project structures, key implementation milestones, and compliance with the established participation terms.

If you are a venture or strategic investor considering participation exceeding 50% of the investment pool, an individual engagement format may be arranged.

NoctAI operates as an investment-oriented project origination and participation platform. The platform itself does not offer securities, does not act as a broker-dealer, investment advisor, or fund, and does not provide financial or profit guarantees.

Any references to investment logic relate to the underlying projects, not to direct user actions on this site.
