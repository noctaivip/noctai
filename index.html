Institutional access to private company equity

Curated access to minority stakes in private companies

NoctAI is a structured access platform where selected companies present minority equity participation to a controlled network of investors.

The platform is designed for curated private-company deal access, not for open public fundraising. Each opportunity is individually reviewed, structured, and presented under controlled access conditions.

All participation is executed outside the platform through direct legal agreements between founders and investors.

Admission criteria

Projects listed on NoctAI must meet platform admission requirements:

• Registered legal entity
• Defined ownership structure
• Documented minority equity allocation
• Founder identity confirmation

Unverified or unstructured offers are not admitted to the platform.

Platform model

NoctAI acts as a structured access layer between founders offering equity participation and investors seeking private-company opportunities.

The platform does not sell securities, does not act as a broker, and does not process investment transactions.

All participation arrangements are executed directly between founders and investors outside the platform.
